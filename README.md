🌍 Global Fuel Prices and Taxation Trends: A Comprehensive Analysis

An Interactive Data Analytics and Visualization Project Using Tableau Public

---

📌 1. Project Overview

Fuel prices play an important role in the global economy, influencing transportation, trade, industrial production, household expenses, and the cost of living. However, fuel prices vary considerably across countries and regions because of differences in taxation policies, government subsidies, economic conditions, market structures, and other influencing factors.

The Global Fuel Prices and Taxation Trends project explores these variations through data analysis and interactive visualizations created using Tableau Public.

The project examines petrol, diesel, and LPG prices across countries and global regions over the period from January 2020 to April 2026. It also explores taxation percentages, regional differences, and fuel-price patterns across different income groups.

By combining multiple worksheets, an interactive dashboard, and a structured Tableau Story, this project transforms a large dataset into visual insights that are easier to explore and understand.

🎯 2. Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are:

- To analyze and compare average petrol prices across different countries.
- To examine diesel and LPG price variations across countries and regions.
- To identify changes in petrol and diesel prices over time.
- To compare average fuel prices across seven global regions.
- To explore the relationship between taxation percentages and petrol prices.
- To investigate fuel-price patterns across different income levels.
- To present complex information through interactive charts and dashboards.
- To communicate the findings through a structured Tableau Story.

❓ 3. Problem Statement

Fuel prices differ significantly across countries and regions, making global comparisons difficult without a systematic analytical approach. Taxation, subsidies, economic conditions, regional policies, and market factors may all contribute to these differences.

The main problem addressed by this project is how to organize and visualize global fuel-price and taxation data so that country-level comparisons, regional variations, historical trends, and possible relationships between taxation and fuel prices can be explored effectively.

The project uses Tableau Public to present the available data through charts, an interactive dashboard, and a Story that guides viewers through the analysis.

🔍 6. Project Methodology

The project follows a structured data-analysis workflow.

Step 1: Data Understanding

The available dataset was reviewed to understand its fields, time coverage, country information, fuel-price measures, and taxation-related variables.

Step 2: Data Preparation

Relevant fields were selected for analysis. Country, region, date, fuel-price, and taxation measures were used in the appropriate visualizations. Data types and field roles were considered when preparing charts.

Step 3: Exploratory Analysis

Fuel prices were compared across countries, regions, time periods, and income groups to explore similarities and differences in the dataset.

Step 4: Visualization Development

Individual worksheets were created using suitable chart types, including bar charts, line charts, treemaps, pie charts, stacked bars, and scatter plots.

Step 5: Dashboard Development

The selected visualizations were combined into a consolidated dashboard to provide an overview of the project's major analytical areas.

Step 6: Story Development

A Tableau Story was organized into sections to present the analysis in a logical sequence, from geographic differences and temporal trends to taxation comparisons and the final conclusion.

Step 7: Publishing and Documentation

The workbook was published to Tableau Public, and the project documentation was maintained in GitHub for review and presentation.

📈 7. Detailed Description of Tableau Worksheets

The Tableau workbook contains 11 worksheets, each designed to examine a different aspect of global fuel prices and taxation.

7.1 Average Petrol Price by Country

Chart type: Horizontal bar chart

This visualization compares average petrol prices across countries. It helps viewers identify differences in average petrol prices and compare countries with relatively higher or lower values.

Purpose: To provide a country-level comparison of petrol prices.

7.2 Fuel Price Trends

Chart type: Time-series line chart

This worksheet presents petrol and diesel price movements over time. It allows viewers to explore changes between 2020 and 2026 and identify visible fluctuations in the available data.

Purpose: To examine historical fuel-price movements.

7.3 Regional Fuel Price Comparison

Chart type: Bar chart

This visualization compares average petrol prices across the seven global regions represented in the dataset.

Purpose: To identify differences in average petrol prices between regions.

7.4 Taxation vs Fuel Prices

Chart type: Scatter plot

This worksheet plots taxation-related values against average petrol prices. Each plotted mark represents an observation in the dataset, allowing viewers to explore whether differences in taxation are associated with differences in petrol prices.

Purpose: To investigate possible associations between taxation and petrol prices.

Interpretation note: A visual association does not establish causation. Other economic, policy, and market factors may also influence fuel prices.

7.5 Petrol Price Treemap

Chart type: Treemap

The treemap provides a compact visual comparison of petrol-price values across the represented categories. Differences in the displayed blocks help viewers explore variation within the dataset.

Purpose: To provide an alternative visual representation of petrol-price differences.

7.6 Average Tax Percentage by Region

Chart type: Pie chart

This worksheet displays regional taxation-related percentages as a proportional visual comparison.

Purpose: To explore how the represented tax percentages vary across global regions.

7.7 Diesel Price Trends

Chart type: Time-series line chart

This worksheet focuses on diesel-price movements over time, helping viewers explore changes in the recorded average prices across the study period.

Purpose: To examine temporal patterns in diesel prices.

7.8 LPG Price Trends

Chart type: Time-series line chart

This visualization presents changes in LPG prices over time. It provides a separate view of LPG trends rather than combining them with petrol or diesel.

Purpose: To examine changes in LPG prices during the study period.

7.9 Diesel Price by Country

Chart type: Bar chart

This worksheet compares average diesel-price values across countries represented in the visualization.

Purpose: To explore country-level differences in diesel prices.

7.10 LPG Price by Region

Chart type: Bar chart

This visualization compares LPG-price values across global regions, allowing viewers to examine regional differences in the available observations.

Purpose: To analyze regional variation in LPG prices.

7.11 Fuel Prices by Income Level

Chart type: Stacked bar chart

This worksheet groups fuel-price information by income-level categories. It provides a visual comparison of fuel-price patterns across the represented groups.

Purpose: To explore differences in fuel-price measures across income levels.

💡 10. Key Analytical Observations

The visualizations are designed to support the following types of observations:

1. Country-level variation: Average petrol and diesel prices differ across the countries represented in the dataset.
2. Regional differences: Comparisons reveal variation in fuel-price measures across the seven global regions.
3. Temporal movement: Time-series charts make it possible to explore changes and fluctuations between 2020 and 2026.
4. Fuel-type differences: Petrol, diesel, and LPG can be examined separately to understand their different patterns.
5. Taxation-related variation: The scatter plot allows viewers to explore possible associations between taxation measures and petrol prices.
6. Income-group comparisons: Grouping fuel-price information by income level supports comparisons across the available categories.

These observations describe the analytical questions supported by the workbook. Specific numerical conclusions should be drawn from the actual chart values and dataset rather than assumed from the visual design alone.

⚠️ 12. Limitations

- The analysis is based on the variables and observations available in the dataset.
- Data coverage and completeness may vary across countries, regions, fuel types, and dates.
- The visualizations are primarily descriptive and comparative.
- A relationship between taxation and fuel prices does not prove that taxation alone causes price differences.
- Additional economic indicators and statistical methods would be needed to investigate causal relationships more rigorously.
